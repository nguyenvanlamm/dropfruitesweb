Upcoming Version 1.0.0 iPhone

First release

The first version of Fruit Mischief for the App Store: two baskets on a balancing pole, an 8-fruit tropical chain, and a blessing draft on top.

The game

  • A street vendor carries two baskets on a bamboo shoulder pole. Every fruit goes into the left or the right basket, and only fruit in the same basket merge.
  • The pole tips toward the heavier basket. Held past its limit for 2.5 seconds, the load spills and the run ends. While it tips, the pole glows red and arrows point at the lighter side.
  • An 8-tier chain of tropical fruit from kumquat to durian, all original art drawn in the game's code. The fruit worry when a basket fills up and grin when they merge.
  • Two durians in one basket burst for a 200-point bonus and a bonus blessing pick.
  • Combos: merges less than 0.55 seconds apart multiply your score by 1.5 per step.
  • One-finger controls: drag over a basket to aim, release to drop.

Blessings

  • Seven blessings: Magnet, Deep Baskets, Lucky Draw, Combo Master, Cleaver, Hammer, and Swap.
  • Every run opens with a blessing pick before the first fruit falls. The next draft arrives at merge six, and later drafts come further apart.
  • Hold up to three blessings at a time. When your slots are full, drafts come half as often.
  • The board freezes the moment a draft opens, so nothing moves while you choose.

Physics

  • A 120 Hz solver with rolling friction, so fruit rolls and settles naturally in each basket.
  • Fruit resting in a pile goes still and stops jittering or spinning forever.
  • The two halves of a cleaved fruit no longer merge straight back together.

Shop, sound, and polish

  • Optional item packs that add Cleaver, Hammer, and Swap uses. Each purchase is saved to your stock before it's confirmed with the App Store.
  • Sound effects that respect the silent switch and mix with your music.
  • An app icon and splash screen of the vendor carrying the pole, and a Credits screen for every asset's authors.
  • A privacy manifest that declares no tracking and no collected data.
