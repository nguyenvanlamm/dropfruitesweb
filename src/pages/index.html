How it plays

Easy to learn, hard to balance

Every run takes a few minutes. Each drop weighs a merge in one basket against the balance of the whole pole.

  1. Pick a basket

    Drag over the left or the right basket to aim, then let go. The fruit stays in the basket it falls into.

  2. Merge inside a basket

    Two matching fruits in the same basket become the next fruit up. Land merges less than half a second apart for a ×1.5 combo.

  3. Keep the pole level

    The pole tips toward the heavier basket. Past its limit it glows red and arrows point at the lighter side. Leave it tipped for 2.5 seconds and the load spills.

The fruit chain

Kumquat to durian in 11 tiers

Eleven tropical fruits, all drawn for this game. Bigger fruit weigh more, so every merge shifts the pole.

Only the 5 smallest fruits fall from the top. Two durians in one basket burst for +200 points and a bonus blessing pick.
The roguelike part

Seven blessings. Room for three.

Every run opens with a draft of three blessings, before the first fruit falls, and more drafts follow as you merge. When your slots are full, taking a new one means giving one up, so no two runs build the same way.

{{blessings}}

Three slots. No more.

Passive blessings are always on. Active ones have charges that refill as you merge.
