How it plays

Easy to learn, hard to stop

Every run takes a few minutes. The bin gets tighter with every drop, so every decision counts.

  1. Aim and drop

    Drag sideways to line up the next fruit, then let go. Physics does the rest: fruit rolls, stacks, and settles.

  2. Merge up

    Two matching fruits that touch become the next fruit up. Land merges less than half a second apart for a ×1.5 combo.

  3. Choose a blessing

    At merge six the board freezes and offers three blessings. Pick one, and play on. Keep fruit under the line, or the run ends.

The fruit chain

Cherry to Watermelon in 11 tiers

Each merge grows the fruit and your score. The watermelon fills almost the whole bin, so making one takes planning.

Only the 5 smallest fruits fall from the top. Two watermelons meet? Both vanish for +200 points.
The roguelike part

Seven blessings. Room for three.

Each draft deals three blessings at random. When your slots are full, taking a new one means giving one up, so no two runs build the same way.

{{blessings}}

Three slots. No more.

Passive blessings are always on. Active ones have charges that refill as you merge.

Balanced by bot

Every blessing had to earn its slot

A test bot played 20 runs with each blessing and compared them to runs with none. A blessing that scored worse than no blessing counted as a bug, not a trade-off.

The first Lucky Draw dropped bigger fruit. It sounded like a buff, but it filled the bin faster and scored below the baseline. Now it drops fruit you already have on the board, and it helps.

Score multiplier against a no-blessing baseline of 464 points. These are internal bot runs from development, not player data.
